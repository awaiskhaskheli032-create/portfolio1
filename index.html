CH 02

About

I'm an engineering student who got hooked on the idea that a network is really just a promise — that a packet, a call, or a signal will get from A to B reliably, even when the physical world is doing its best to get in the way. That's what pulled me toward Telecommunications Engineering at MUET: the mix of RF fundamentals, protocol design and hands-on infrastructure work.

Outside of coursework, I spend most of my time in two places: routing tables and terminal windows. I'm drawn to networking and telecom systems — building, breaking, and rebuilding small networks in Packet Tracer and GNS3 — and to programming, using Python and C/C++ to automate the boring parts and understand the protocols from the inside out.
